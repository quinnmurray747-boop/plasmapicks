Underdog NFL board, ranked by edge

Pricing the board.

Avg is his per-game season average (games played in brackets) and Last 4 is his last four games. Proj starts from his share of the team's throws or carries, weighted toward recent games, times his per-play efficiency pulled toward the league norm, then adjusts for what this opponent allows to his position, the team's implied points, teammates ruled out and the game script. Chance is an even blend of the simulation and Underdog's own price. Needs is the hit rate that breaks even at that payout. Flagged props (⚑) are left off unless you include them: a changed team or quarterback, an injury tag, under four games, a falling snap share, or a line far from the projection. The model's average lean against Underdog's prices is removed for each prop type, so an edge means this player differs from the market, not that the model runs low or high overall.

Data: weekly player, snap, injury-report and schedule files from the open nflverse project; lines, spreads and totals from Underdog. Not used yet: weather, routes run and air yards, offensive-line and cornerback matchups.
